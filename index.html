About

Fight Fusion is a 2D fighting game against the computer, built with JavaScript and jQuery. Each round lasts 60 seconds: knock your opponent out, or have more life when time runs out. Win 2 rounds to win the match; if the rounds are tied after 3, extra rounds are played, up to 5. Punch, kick, block, jump over kicks, crouch under punches, and save your combos for when they count. Pick Easy, Normal or Hard before you start, and play with the keyboard or the on-screen controls on a phone. All the artwork is drawn for this game.
